CHILDREN'S TRAILS - pathways designed for primary and middle school groups

The proposed activities bring children into relationship with the natural environment through artistic and storytelling acts.

We will play observation and storytelling games. We will draw and trace the textures of natural elements using various natural materials.
We will design Land Art works using natural materials.
We will create an ephemeral art gallery in the woods, allowing us to build a relationship of care and familiarity with the place that hosts us.
