Musicali si cresce

A project dedicated to early childhood, created to offer programmes and events where music and art can be an experience of listening, connection and care.

It was born in 2011 from the meeting of artists from different disciplines: music, theatre and illustration. What unites them is a specific focus on early childhood, an ongoing personal and group research, and an interest in a pedagogy that places the child and relationships at its centre — where care becomes an essential attitude for shaping one's own possible being, and where even silence finds its space.

The projects draw on the different languages of art and aim to offer alternative vocabularies for exploring the world.

For Musicali si cresce I created the logo, the visuals for editorial products, show posters, and images for communication purposes.
