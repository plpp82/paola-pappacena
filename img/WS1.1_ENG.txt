Drawing While Walking

Drawing While Walking is an artistic experience in nature designed to stimulate new ideas and practices in naturalistic settings such as woods and meadows, or within green spaces in urban areas.

During these short walks we will practise observing the landscape, look closely at how things are made and try to reproduce them. We will experiment with artistic techniques and visual storytelling. We will leave a trace of our journey on paper.

Through moments of listening, dialogue and active practice, we will exercise our creative side by experimenting with new ways of seeing and new perspectives.
